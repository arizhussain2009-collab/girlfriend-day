Letter 01
Everything About You
Jaanu,

woh aankhein 🥺

I don't think you realize what you do to me. Like genuinely — tu ek kaam karti hai, ek cheez bolti hai, ek baar has deti hai — aur main andar se literally khatam ho jaata hun. Aur sabse funny part? Tu jaanti bhi nahi. Tu bas exist karti hai aur mera dil already haar jaata hai. Teri aankhein. Yaar. Woh shade of brown — not too dark, not too light — bilkul woh shade jo seedha dil pe lagti hai. Photo mein hi dekha hai abhi tak, aur tab bhi — ek baar dekha aur kuch ruk gaya andar. Jaise dil ne ek second ke liye breathe karna bhool diya. Teri aankhon mein kuch hai jo sirf feel hota hai, describe nahi hota. They don't just look at you — they pull you in, wrap around you, aur phir bahar nahi nikalna chahte. Teri aankhon ki ek photo hai mere paas — main baar baar dekhta hun. Har baar same feeling. Pighal jaata hun. Literally. Teri awaaz. Sabse bada, sabse ganda, sabse khatarnak weak point hai mera. Teri awaaz itni soft hai, itni gentle, itni elegant — ki jab tu bolti hai toh lagta hai baaki duniya ka volume automatically kam ho jaata hai. Sirf teri awaaz reh jaati hai. Main literally carried away ho jaata hun. Aur yeh jo teri aadat hai — gusse mein bhi chillana nahi, softly bolna — woh aur bhi zyada khatarnak hai. Tu apni awaaz se mujhse kuch bhi karwa sakti hai. Koi complaint nahi 😭 Teri smile. Teri lips. Yahan main rok leta hun khud ko — but just know — dekhte hi literally screen se nikal ke — okay nahi nahi 😄 Par teri smile dekh ke duniya thodi better lagti hai, seriously. Woh wali smile jo dobara dekhne pe majboor kar de. Main karta bhi hun — baar baar — sirf usi wajah se. Tere haath. Koi reason nahi, koi logic nahi — bas pasand hain. Bohot zyada. Shayad isliye ki jo cheez hum chhoo nahi sakte usse aur gehraai se feel karte hain. Ek din haath maangne ki himmat karunga — ya seedha pakad lunga bina poochhe, dekhte hain 😏 Teri personality. Har din thoda aur zyada fall karta hun. Koi ceiling nahi, koi limit nahi. Tu perfect nahi hai — koi nahi hota. Par mere liye perfect hai. Teri har kami, teri har quirk — sab pasand hai. Sab. Bina kisi condition ke. Teri presence. Just the way you exist, Zikra. Tera hona — teri looks, tera andaaz, tera woh aura — kisi ne meri saari duaayein li aur ek insaan bana diya. Cherry on the cake nahi — tu hi poora cake hai, poori bakery hai 😭🖤 Tujhse pyaar karta hun jaanu. Teri aankhon se. Tere haathon se. Teri awaaz se. Teri har us cheez se jo tu hai. Sab kuch. Har cheez. Tu. Tera, Kuchupuchu 🖤

The Confession
Jaanu,Ek cheez hai jo maine kabhi seedha nahi boli. Aaj bol raha hun. Tu yaad kar — jab teri khala admit thi. Woh waqt. Tujhe kitna mushkil tha, kitna dard tha — aur main door tha, sirf messages pe, sirf awaaz pe. Kuch kar nahi sakta tha. Kuch fix nahi kar sakta tha. Bas tha. Usi waqt hua. Main pehle bhi tera tha — ya socha tha ki hun. Par woh waqt tha jab sach mein pada. Jab tujhe toote dekha, jab tune struggle kiya — tab pehli baar dil ne kuch aisa kaha jo main ignore nahi kar saka. Na chaha bhi. Woh feeling strange thi. Ek banda jo kabhi kisi ke liye ruka nahi — woh ruk gaya. Ek banda jisne kabhi kisi ki fikr mein neend nahi khoyi — uski neend gayi. Tere liye. Usi raat. Aur phir teri khala chali gayi. Aur tujhe aur tootte dekha. Aur main aur gira tujhpe. Kyunki jab koi apne dard mein bhi itni dignity se khadi rehti hai — toh usse pyaar karna rukta nahi. Yeh confession hai meri, Zikra. Main tab pada tha tujhpe. Tab tune jaane anjane mujhe hamesha ke liye pakad liya. Tera, Kuchupuchu 🖤

That Day You Proposed
Jaanu,Bakr Eid. Doosra din. Tune propose kiya tha. Main jaanta hun — woh ek ajeeb waqt tha. Abhi abhi breakup hua tha. Shayad hormones bol rahe the, shayad tu khud sure nahi thi. Par main tujhe kuch batata hun — us din jab tune woh kaha, mujhe koi farq nahi pada ki reason kya tha. Maine haan kaha — aur woh haan dil se nikla tha. Haan woh waqt perfect nahi tha. Par kuch cheezein perfect waqt ka intezaar nahi karti. Tu ne propose kiya aur kuch mere andar settle ho gaya — jaise koi cheez apni jagah aa gayi ho. Woh din mujhe hamesha yaad rahega. Na isliye ki woh filmy tha — balki isliye ki usi din se woh Ariz bana jo aaj hai. Jo tujhe chahta hai. Jo tere bina nahi sochta. Teri woh himmat — messy waqt mein bhi apni feeling pe act karna — woh mujhe aaj bhi inspire karti hai. Shukriya us din ke liye jaanu. Seriously. Tera, Kuchupuchu 🖤

What You Don't Know You Do To Me
Jaanu,Tujhe pata bhi nahi hoga — par jo tu randomly karta hai, woh mujhe sabse zyada affect karta hai. Koi plan nahi, koi occasion nahi — bas ek random moment mein tu kuch karti hai aur mera poora din badal jaata hai. Woh random affection. Jab achanak se kuch bolti hai jo mujhe feel karaata hai ki main khaas hun tere liye — woh feeling describe nahi hoti. Main hard exterior wala banda hun baahar se. Par andar? Ek random sweet moment aur main akele kamre mein muskura raha hota hun 😭 Tujhe kuch bada nahi karna mujhe khush karne ke liye. Koi grand gesture nahi chahiye. Bas tera hona, tera randomly yaad karna, tera ek chota sa "hi" — woh bahut hai. Bahut zyada hai. Tu shayad sochti hogi main notice nahi karta. Main sab notice karta hun. Har cheez. Har ek. Tera, Kuchupuchu 🖤

Someday, Inshallah

Kabhi kabhi bohot karta hai. Par phir main sochta hun — aage. Ek din hoga jab yeh sab nahi hoga. Jab milna mushkil nahi hoga. Jab saath baith ke baat kar sakte hain bina kisi darr ke. Bas ek baar sab theek ho jaaye — phir dekh kaise duniya dikhaata hun tujhe 🖤 Tera hamesha, Kuchupuchu 🖤
